# ❄️ M Group Cool ERP & CRM - Enterprise HVAC Final System Report

This document summarizes the comprehensive upgrades, financial controls, and client relationship modules built into the **M Group Cool** advanced ERP & CRM platform.

---

## 📅 1. Business Management & Financial Dashboard (الإدارة المالية والتدفقات)
- **Multi-Register Ledger (إدارة الخزائن الموزعة)**: Fully interactive ledger for Cash registers (Main, Technical Depot) & corporate bank accounts (البنك الأهلي المصري).
- **Partner Withdrawals & Dividends (مسحوبات وتوزيع أرباح الشركاء)**:
  - Accountable profiles for **Mohamed Ashraf** and **Mahmoud**.
  - Dynamic calculations for partnership profit ratios: **50/50**, **40/60**, and **30/70**.
- **Live Cash Flow Tracking (مراقبة التدفق المالي)**: Real-time calculation of Daily and Monthly cash flows with visual balance trackers.

---

## 🧾 2. Quotation Center (مركز عروض الأسعار والتحويل)
- **Professional Quotations (عروض الأسعار الفنية)**: Full lifecycle registry tracking **Pending, Accepted, and Rejected** quotation states.
- **Instant Core Orders Conversion (التوليد التلقائي للطلبات)**:
  - **Convert to Invoice**: Automatically transition quotations into active sales invoices.
  - **Convert to Installation Order**: Instantly file an installation request and dispatch field personnel.
  - **Convert to Maintenance Order**: Immediately schedule cyclical preventive washes and inspections.

---

## 📊 3. Marketing ROI & Customer Acquisition (المصاريف والقنوات التسويقية)
- **Attribution Mapping (تتبع مصادر العملاء)**: Seamless registration tagging channels: *Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, Google, Referral, or Walk-in*.
- **Integrated Campaign Dashboard (تقرير عائد الاستثمار ROI)**: Interactive visual graphs linking marketing spends versus raw acquisition totals, calculation of exact Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), and channel-by-channel conversion stats.

---

## 🛡️ 4. Advanced Warranty Management (إدارة الضمانات المتقدمة)
- **Bimodal Coverage Tracking (الضمان التجاري والمصنعي)**: Comprehensive tracking separating **Company (M Group Cool)** and **Manufacturer (Carrier, Sharp, etc.)** warranty terms.
- **Interactive Warranty Timelines**:
  - Expiry alarm flags based on coverage status.
  - Quick filters for active vs expired guarantees.

---

## 🏭 5. Supply Chain & Supplier Accounting (أرصدة الموردين وصرف الدفعات)
- **Live Ledger & Balances (دفاتر حسابات الموردين)**: Precise dynamic balance calculation (credit/debit states) mapping supplier raw materials vs historical payment clearances.
- **Interactive Expenditure Sync**: Recording supplier payments automatically deducts the funds from specified Cash registers or Bank accounts, ensuring 100% financial consistency.

---

## 🚚 6. Asset & Fleet Tracking (سجل الأصول والسيارات والعهدة)
- **Enterprise Assets Registry (سجل الأصول والعهدة)**: Structured files tracking company property (laptops, high-vacuum pumps, copper benders).
- **Technician Custody Dispatch**: Complete record mapping assignment histories of tools to specific technicians.
- **Vehicle Fleet Maintenance (مركبات الدورية والتوزيع)**: Log monitoring license renewals, insurance expiry dates, and driver schedules, supported by bright visual alerts for near-expiry situations.

---

## 🤖 7. Bilingual AI Assistant & Voice Commands (المساعد الصوتي والذكائي)
- **AI Business Analyst**: Implemented full-stack proxy routing context maps directly through the **Gemini API** using `@google/genai` to deliver real-time metrics (best technicians, expiring contracts, and profit optimization tips).
- **Dual-Language Voice assistant (المساعد الصوتي الثنائي)**: Complete voice simulation triggers supporting **Arabic and English** keyboard/vocal commands (Adding clients, generating invoices, checking profit margins).
